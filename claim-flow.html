CGPE Health Claim — Bot ka poora flow

Intimation se lekar settlement tak · 22 July 2026
bana hua, chal raha hai tukde hain, jodna hai zero se banana hai

1 Intimation bana hua

  • Client ya team WhatsApp pe claim intimate karta hai
  • Bot claim record banata hai — status intimated
  • Chhota claim ID milta hai (CLM-1105)

2 Record check aadha

  • Client DB mein naam / policy number dhoondo — 9,012 records
  • Insurer aur TPA pehchano — 21 TPA + 31 insurer master maujood
Mil gaya → policy details uthao, aage badho
Nahi mila → team ko flag, manually verify

3 Email intimation banana hai

  • Insurer ko intimation email
  • Cashless ho to TPA ko bhi
  • Email address master se uthao
  • Bheja hua email claim record mein log ho

4 24 ghante ka follow-up banana hai

Reply aaya → claim / CIR number save karo
Reply nahi → reminder email + team ko alert

5 Document collection bana hua

  • Client / hospital se documents WhatsApp pe aate hain
  • Har file archive hoti hai aur claim se judi rehti hai
  • 20 MB+ file apne aap tukdon mein bat kar process hoti hai

6 OCR + analysis bana hua

  • Gemini OCR — text aur bill ki har line alag
  • Policy terms file se hi padh leta hai (schedule ho to)
  • Har line: payable / non-payable / room-linked
  • Deduction waterfall — IRDAI ke niyam
  • Bill ka chhapa total vs lines ka jod — milaake dekhta hai

7 Submit se pehle verdict bana hua

  • 23-point document checklist
  • Waiting period / exclusion / policy active check
  • “Client se ye mangwayein” — seedha forward karne laayak list
Blocker hai → SUBMIT MAT KARO, kami poori karo
File saaf hai → aage badho

8 Submit + tracking banana hai

  • File submit (abhi manual)
  • TPA se query / IR aaye to ticket bane aur apne aap chase ho
  • 15 din tak status track ho

9 Settlement + seekhna banana hai

  • Settlement amount record ho
  • Jo socha tha vs jo mila — compare
  • Farak ki wajah note ho
  • Yahi se asli data banega, jisse aage ka andaaza sudhrega

Sabse bada gap — EMAIL

Aaj tak ek bhi email nahi gaya — 0 claims. Email directory mein sirf 5 entry hain, jabki chahiye 52 (31 insurer + 21 TPA).

Poora flow email par tikka hai. Iske bina Stage 3, 4 aur 8 shuru hi nahi ho sakte.

Ek faisla chahiye

Abhi document Gemini padhta hai, aur uske jawab ko humari rulebook (IRDAI ke niyam, code mein likhe hue) cross-check karti hai.

Claude ki health-claim-review skill ke niyam code mein utaare gaye hain, par skill khud nahi chalti.

Agar analysis Claude se karwana ho — jo gande scan aur ajeeb bill-heads par behtar padhega — to ho sakta hai, par uspe API ka kharcha aayega. Abhi sab kuch free hai.

Aage ka kram — sujhav

  1. Email base — directory bharo, intimation + follow-up  (Stage 3, 4)
  2. Query tracking — TPA query = ticket, apne aap chase  (Stage 8)
  3. Settlement loop — jo mila woh record karo, compare karo, seekho  (Stage 9)
  4. Admission alert — room limit ki warning admission ke din, bill aane ke baad nahi  (naya)

#4 sabse zyada paisa bachayega. Bill aane ke baad room ka nuksan ho chuka hota hai — koi tool wapas nahi la sakta. Engine tayyar hai, sirf chhota flow banana hai.
