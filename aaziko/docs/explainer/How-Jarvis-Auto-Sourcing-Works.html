📩
STEP 1

A buyer email arrives

👉 What it does
A buyer writes in asking to source a product.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
It lands in the sales mailbox, or you paste it into the panel's “⚙️ Auto-sourcing” box.
📍 In our real run
Buyer: “We want to source cotton T-shirts, 180 GSM combed, GOTS certified, ~10,000 pcs, delivered to Hamburg. Please share pricing.”
🧾
STEP 2

Understand the requirement

👉 What it does
Jarvis reads the email and pulls out exactly what the buyer needs.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
AI reads the text and extracts product, quantity, specs, destination — only what the buyer actually wrote, never invents.
📍 In our real run
product = cotton T-shirts · qty = 10,000 · specs = 180 GSM combed, GOTS · destination = Hamburg
🔢
STEP 3

Resolve the HS code

👉 What it does
Turns the product name into its 6-digit HS code — the universal ID used in global trade & customs.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
The Product Resolver matches the name against our HS-code database (and disambiguates — e.g. “keyboard” = computer vs musical).
📍 In our real run
cotton T-shirts → HS 610910
🏭
STEP 4

Find & score Indian suppliers

👉 What it does
Finds real Indian exporters of this product and ranks the strongest.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
Looks up 107,872 real India exporters (customs data) by HS, scores each by export volume + value. If none are found, it stops honestly — no fake quote.
📍 In our real run
FAIR EXPORTS INDIA [78] · SHAHI EXPORTS [63] · BINDU FASHION [29]
✉️
STEP 5

Draft RFQs to suppliers

👉 What it does
Writes a Request-for-Quotation to each top supplier.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
AI writes each RFQ in Aaziko's style — asking ex-works/FOB price, MOQ, lead-time, payment terms & samples. Saved to the review queue; nothing is sent.
📍 In our real run
3 RFQ drafts ready for a human to review & send.
💬
STEP 6

Collect the quotes

👉 What it does
Reads each supplier's reply and pulls out the numbers.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
AI extracts price, currency, MOQ, lead-time, validity from the reply. (In the demo a placeholder quote is used — real supplier emails turn this on.)
📍 In our real run
[simulated] USD 3.20 / pc · MOQ 3,000 · 30–35 days
🧮
STEP 7

Calculate the landed cost

👉 What it does
Works out what the buyer actually pays at their port.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
supplier price + import duty (from the tariff database) + freight → computed by code, not AI, so the numbers are exact.
📍 In our real run
3.20 + 0.26 freight → USD 3.46 / pc CIF Hamburg
📝
STEP 8

Prepare the quotation

👉 What it does
Writes the final quotation email to the buyer.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
AI writes it using only the computed numbers (never invents a price). Saved to the review queue; nothing is sent.
📍 In our real run
“Landed-cost USD 3.46/pc CIF Hamburg… samples ~25 days… valid 30 days… confirm → proforma invoice.”
📤
STEP 9

Send — but gated

👉 What it does
A human approves, then it goes out.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
The gated send only lets allow-listed test addresses through; a real buyer is blocked until senior switches it on. No email leaves by mistake.
📍 In our real run
Reviewer clicks Approve & Send → (gated) → sent.
🔎
STEP 10

Track the response

👉 What it does
When the buyer replies, Jarvis decides if it's a yes or a no.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
An AI classifier reads the reply and labels it positive / negative / neutral.
📍 In our real run
“The landed price works, please proceed” → POSITIVE
✅
STEP 11

Update the records

👉 What it does
Closes the loop based on the answer.
⚙️ How (under the hood)
Positive → mark the CRM opportunity won. Negative → add the buyer to the newsletter list for future nurture. (Staged in our DB; real CRM/newsletter writes flip on with senior approval.)
📍 In our real run
POSITIVE → CRM opportunity marked won (staged).
